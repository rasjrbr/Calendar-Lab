iPhone · iOS 26+

Your roster, organized and checked against the rules.

Calendar Lab turns your iFlightNeo roster into a clean, enriched calendar with duty limits, home and airport standby rules, and rest alerts. All processing happens on your iPhone.

On your device

No Calendar Lab servers. No account, no ads, no tracking, no usage analytics.

A clean calendar

Reads the iFlightNeo calendar (read-only) and writes organized events to the Calendar-Lab calendar.

Rules and limits

Duty limits, Home Standby (HSB), Airport Standby (ASB) and Red Eye rules, evaluated in Brasília time.

Crew and flights

Crew list per leg, who joins and who leaves, aircraft registrations, and quick access to company portals.

Calendar Lab is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by LATAM Airlines, IBS Software (iFlightNeo), ANAC or any other company mentioned. The app's information is for support only: the official roster and company and ANAC rules always prevail.
