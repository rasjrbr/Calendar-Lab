Frequently asked questions

Does Calendar Lab send my roster to a server?

No. The App has no servers and processes your roster on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the App change the iFlightNeo calendar?

No. The source calendar is only read. Organized events are written only to the Calendar-Lab calendar.

How do I delete my data?

For the iCloud backup: the App's Settings › Security & Privacy › “Delete iCloud Backup”. For local data: the App's Settings › Reset or Fix App › “Full App Reset”, or delete the App.

Are the App's calculated limits official?

No. They are for information only. The official roster and company and ANAC rules always prevail. See the Terms of Use.
